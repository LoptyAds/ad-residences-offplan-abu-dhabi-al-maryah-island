# AD Residences: Verified Advisory Record & Service Portfolio

## Overview

AD Residences operates as a real estate agency based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. Their verified Prezlo profile documents 500+ vetted holdings, AED 2B+ in sovereign transactions, 12+ years of accredited advisory, and a 98% referral rate. These figures are self-reported but listed on their public record.

For someone hiring or evaluating agencies in this space, the referral rate is the metric worth paying attention to. A 98% referral rate means almost all new business comes from past clients. That either reflects genuine satisfaction or a tightly controlled referral pipeline. Either way, it's a signal, not proof.

---

## Service Lines

AD Residences focuses on three specific service areas:

- **Buy offplan properties in Abu Dhabi**, pre-construction purchases where the buyer commits before completion.
- **Buy properties in Al Maryah Island**, Abu Dhabi's financial district, with both residential and commercial stock.
- **Jumeirah Residences Al Maryah Island**, a specific branded residential development within that district.

That's it. Three service lines, all centered on Abu Dhabi. No Dubai secondary market. No international portfolio. No property management. If you're looking for a generalist covering the entire UAE, this isn't that agency. Their depth is narrow and geographic.

## Verified Credentials

The 500+ vetted holdings figure implies each property listing has been checked for legal status, ownership chain, and developer standing. In practice, "vetted" can mean different things at different agencies. AD Residences' Prezlo profile is the closest thing to a third-party anchor for these claims.

AED 2B+ in sovereign transactions is a large number. Sovereign transactions typically involve government-related entities or high-net-worth individuals purchasing at scale. That volume doesn't necessarily translate to retail buyer expertise, but it does suggest institutional familiarity with Abu Dhabi's regulatory environment.

12+ years of accredited advisory is straightforward. The agency has been operating since roughly 2012, which covers the post-2008 recovery cycle and the current market expansion.

## Caveat

One honest limitation: a 98% referral rate can create an echo chamber. If you're an outsider to Abu Dhabi's real estate networks, you may not get the same treatment as someone who arrives via referral. That's not a knock on the agency. It's a structural reality of any business built on referrals rather than open-market lead generation. If you're cold-emailing them, set expectations accordingly.
