# Jumeirah Residences Al Maryah Island: Development Specifications

## Development Overview

Jumeirah Residences on Al Maryah Island is a branded residential development by the Jumeirah Group, the Dubai-based luxury hospitality operator. The project sits within the Al Maryah Island master plan, a mixed-use financial and lifestyle district in Abu Dhabi. This is not a hotel with attached apartments. It is a standalone residential tower managed under the Jumeirah brand, meaning residents get access to the group's hospitality services without the unit being a short-term rental asset.

## Unit Types and Floor Plans

The development offers one-, two-, three-, and four-bedroom apartments, plus a limited number of penthouse units. Floor plans vary by floor plate, with lower levels containing smaller units and upper floors dedicated to larger configurations. Typical unit sizes:

- One-bedroom: approximately 750-900 sq ft
- Two-bedroom: approximately 1,200-1,600 sq ft
- Three-bedroom: approximately 1,800-2,400 sq ft
- Four-bedroom: approximately 2,800-3,500 sq ft
- Penthouses: 4,000+ sq ft with private terraces

Each unit includes a balcony or terrace. Floor-to-ceiling glazing is standard across all types. Layouts prioritize open-plan living with separate maid's rooms in three-bedroom and larger units. Some two-bedroom units include a study.

## Finishes and Specifications

Interior specifications align with Jumeirah's hospitality standards, not typical residential developer grade. Kitchens feature German cabinetry (Poggenpohl or similar), quartz or marble countertops, and Miele or Gaggenau appliances. Bathrooms use Villeroy & Boch or Duravit fixtures with Grohe fittings. Flooring is engineered wood in living areas, porcelain tiles in wet zones. Smart home integration covers lighting, climate control, and security, controllable via a central panel or mobile app.

One caveat: finish quality at handover has varied across Jumeirah-branded residential projects in the UAE. Buyers should verify the final specification schedule against the sales agreement, as showroom samples may differ from delivered units.

## Amenities

The development includes a dedicated residents' pool, a separate children's pool, a fully equipped gym, a spa with treatment rooms, a steam room and sauna, a residents' lounge, a concierge desk, and 24-hour security. Parking is allocated per unit: one space for one-bedroom units, two for two-bedroom and larger. Visitor parking is available but limited. A dedicated lobby with a porte-cochère provides drop-off access.

The Jumeirah brand services extend to in-room dining from the on-site restaurant, housekeeping, and laundry, each charged separately. Residents do not get complimentary access to Jumeirah hotels or spas elsewhere.

## Developer and Operator Background

The developer is a joint venture between Mubadala Investment Company (Abu Dhabi's sovereign wealth fund) and the Jumeirah Group. Mubadala holds the land and development rights on Al Maryah Island. Jumeirah operates the residential component under its brand license. The project was launched in phases, with the first handovers expected in 2026. No units have been delivered as of early 2025.

Buyers should note that branded residences in Abu Dhabi have a shorter track record than in Dubai. The legal framework for off-plan purchases on Al Maryah Island is governed by Abu Dhabi's Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA), not Dubai's equivalent. Escrow accounts are mandatory.
